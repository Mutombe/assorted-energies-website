05 · Questions

Questions we hear
on every site visit.

Does it run during load-shedding?

Yes, if it includes batteries and a hybrid inverter. Grid-tied systems without storage shut off in an outage for safety. We size batteries to your loads.

How long does an install take?

Most homes take one to three days once the design is signed off. Larger projects run to an agreed programme.

Do you service other installs?

Yes. We inspect, service and repair systems built by others, starting with a health check and a written report.

Can you help with ZERA?

Yes. We prepare the technical requirements for a ZERA Solar IPP generation licence: studies, drawings and yield reports.

Can I buy equipment only?

Yes. Panels, Victron inverters and batteries, lithium storage, controllers and solar lights. Call for stock.
