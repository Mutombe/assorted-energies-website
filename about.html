Our story

01 · Overture

Power is our whole practice.

You need an energy partner who thinks like an engineer.

If power runs your home, farm or business, you need a team that designs it, builds it and answers for it. Panels on a roof are the easy part.

Renewable power is all we do. We deliver EPC and O&M for utility-scale solar, biomass, hydro, IGCC and thermal plants, and bring the same rigour to every home.

We also supply what we install: panels, Victron inverters and batteries, lithium storage, charge controllers and solar lights.

And we help developers meet the requirements for a ZERA Solar IPP generation licence.

  • Solar PV
  • Battery storage
  • Biomass
  • Hydro
  • IGCC
  • Thermal
  • Solar lighting

02 · What sets us apart

What makes our work
hold up for decades.

These three strengths run through every project, from a 3 kWp home kit to a multi-megawatt plant.

Local, regional, international

Harare roots with project experience across borders, so you get global standards with local know-how on permits, suppliers and site conditions.

In-house engineering

Design, yield modelling, electrical and structural work done by our own engineers, so the people who quote are the people who answer for it.

Lifetime O&M

A continuous loop of monitoring, cleaning, servicing and repair that keeps output close to day-one performance for decades.

03 · What we stand for

Values that keep
the lights on.

Energy is infrastructure, and infrastructure is trust. These are the commitments we hold ourselves to on every site.

Responsiveness

We pick up the phone, show up on time and fix faults fast. A system you can't get support for isn't a reliable system.

Engineering integrity

Correct cable sizing, proper earthing, honest yield estimates. We do the unseen work properly because that's what lasts.

Lasting impact

Clean, sustainable and reliable power that lowers costs for families and businesses and cuts emissions for everyone.
